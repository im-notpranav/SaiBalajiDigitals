A2. Signing in, profile and notifications

Sign in Login page

  1. Open the company OMS link given to you by the Administrator. It works on a computer and on a phone.
  2. Enter your username and password. Both are given to you by the Administrator.
  3. Press Sign in. The system opens your own home page. Each role has a different home page.

You stay signed in for 7 days on that device. Use Sign out at the bottom of the menu on shared computers.

Your profile Menu › Profile

  • Change your password. Enter the current password, then the new one.
  • Profile photo. Upload a photo of yourself (up to 512 KB). You can also remove it.
  • WhatsApp messages. You can stop or allow WhatsApp messages. You still get every in-app notification.
  • Name, username, email and mobile are set by the Administrator. You cannot change them yourself. Ask the Administrator if one is wrong.

Notifications Menu › Notifications

The system tells you when something needs you or when your work moves on. Notifications come three ways:

ChannelHow it works
In-appAlways on. The Notifications page shows every message. Open one to mark it read.
WhatsAppSent to the mobile number on your account. Each message has an Open in app button that takes you straight to the item. If you are signed out, you sign in first and then land on the item.
EmailOrder events (created, edited, flagged, billed) and Excel exports go to the email on your account.
Good to knowWhatsApp never holds up your work. If a WhatsApp message fails or your number is missing, the action still saves and the in-app notification still arrives.

Using the system on a phone

  • Office roles: tap the menu button (three lines) at the top left to open the menu.
  • Field staff: a tab bar at the bottom shows Work, Leave, Payments, Alerts and Profile.
  • Light and dark themes are both available. Your choice is remembered.
  • If a page looks out of date, reload it. Another person may have changed it.

A3. Words used in the system

WordMeaning
OrderOne customer job. It has an order number like ORD260145: ORD, the year the financial year started (26 = April 2026 to March 2027), and a running number. Numbers restart each financial year.
StoreOne location inside an order (store name plus location). An order has 1 to 50 stores. Each store can have its own PO number.
Line itemOne printed piece on a store: media, width and height in inches, quantity and rate.
SFTSquare feet. The system works it out: Width × Height × Qty ÷ 144. Amount = SFT × Rate.
Job PO / Store POThe customer's purchase order number for the whole order, or for one store.
FlagA mark on a line item that says "this line is wrong". A flagged line cannot be finished by production until an Admin resolves the flag.
RemarkThe billing reason on a line: Normal, Reprint, Sample, Under Warranty, Free of Cost, Revised, Extra Amount, Less Amount or Other. See C3.
InvoiceA bill raised by Accounts on one or more stores of an order.
Field jobWork given to a field team (Pasters, Helpers, Driver) for set dates, usually to install an order.
CSM codeThe CSM's employee code (for example 300). It shows on the Weekly Grid so everyone knows whose job it is.
Payment requestMoney the company pays to you by UPI for work expenses (travel, material, labour, food, advance, other). It is not customer money.
UTRThe bank reference number of a UPI payment.
Working daysMonday to Saturday. Sundays are not counted in any timer or overdue count.
Financial year (FY)1 April to 31 March.
OverdueWaiting longer than the allowed number of working days. Shown in red.

Step by step

Start an order for a CSM Orders › Start order

  1. Press Start order.
  2. Enter the client and pick the CSM (only active CSMs are listed).
  3. Keep the next order number, or type a custom number for this order.
  4. Save. The CSM is told and adds the stores and line items.

If the number is taken you see That order number is already used. Pick another.

Edit an order and resolve flags Orders › open order

  1. Open the order. You can edit the header, stores and lines at any status. A CSM cannot edit settled orders, but you can.
  2. A flagged line shows the CSM's reason. Press Remove Flag once the line is dealt with, or Reject flag with a note to the CSM.
  3. To correct a saved line, change its size, quantity or rate and choose remark Revised with a reason NEW.
  4. To take a line off the bill, set its remark to Reprint, Sample, Under Warranty, Free of Cost or Other.

Decide an amount change request NEW Order › Amount changes

  1. A CSM asks for an Extra Amount or Less Amount on a line or on the whole order. You get a notification.
  2. Open the order. Check the reason and the figure the CSM suggests. You can change the figure.
  3. For Less Amount, choose whether you enter the new amount or the discount.
  4. Approve, or Reject with a reason. The CSM is told either way.

If the order is already invoiced and the change breaks the billed total, the order moves to Pending and Accounts is told.

Close or force-close an order Order › Actions

  1. Close works only on a Pending order (billed or paid short). Choose a remark and add text if you pick Other.
  2. Force close works at any status. Use it when an order must stop, for example a sample or warranty job. Choose the reason.
  3. The order becomes Completed. This is final.
Before you closeCheck for open field jobs and payment requests on the order. Closing does not cancel them.

Review a payment request (Admin stage) Payment Requests

  1. Open a request in Admin review. Check the order, amount, purpose, payee UPI and any files.
  2. Choose one: Approve (goes to Cost Control), Return for info (reason required; goes back to the requester), or Reject (reason required; final).
  3. While a request is under review you may also Cancel it with a reason.

Requests above the Admin review limit (default ₹5,000) always come to you first, whatever bill option was chosen.

Manage users Users · super admin

  1. Add a user. Enter name, username, mobile, email, password and role. Mobile and email are required.
  2. To fix someone's details, edit the user. Staff cannot change their own username, email or mobile.
  3. To stop access, switch the user inactive. They lose access on their next action. Prefer this to deleting: users with history are kept for the audit trail.
  4. To reset a password, use Reset password and give the new password to the person privately.

Settings Settings

SettingWhat it does
Order Number SequenceShows the current prefix and next number. Adjust Next Order Number sets it. Lock sequence adjustments prevents manual overrides.
Payment request overdue limitWorking days before a waiting request turns red. Whole number 1 to 90. Default 10.
Admin review limit for payment requestsRequests above this amount always start with the Admin. Default ₹5,000. Exactly ₹5,000 follows the normal route.

Bulk Import Bulk Import · super admin

  1. Download the order sheet template. Fill one order per client.
  2. Upload. The system shows a preview with any errors per row.
  3. Fix errors and upload again. Import is all or nothing: if one row fails, nothing is saved.

Serial No, Order No, Total SFT and Amount in the sheet are ignored. The system works them out.

WhatsApp log WhatsApp

Every message has a status: Queued, Sent, Delivered, Read, Failed, Skipped or Logged. Skipped means the person opted out or has no valid mobile. Logged means WhatsApp is in test mode and nothing was sent. Use Resend on Failed or Skipped messages after fixing the cause.

Limits

The system will not let you
  • Close an order that is not Pending. Use Force close instead.
  • Delete an order that has payment requests, field jobs or a correction history. Close it instead.
  • Mark a payment request paid or closed while an active Cost Control Manager exists. That is their job.
  • Correct a payout after it is marked paid. Bounced or wrong payments are fixed outside the system with Accounts.
  • Undo a Completed order or a Cancelled field job.

Step by step

Assign a field job Dashboard › Assign Work

  1. Dates. Pick the start date and end date. Required. One job runs whole days, at most 62 days.
  2. CSM. Pick the CSM. Required. Choosing an order fills it in for you.
  3. Order. Optional. Pick the order and, if you like, which of its stores. The order can be linked later by you, the Admin or the job's CSM.
  4. Location. Optional: Hyderabad or Non-local, plus an area name. Type freely; suggestions come from the Area master and new names are added to it.
  5. Team. Pick 1 or 2 Pasters, exactly 1 Driver, and set Helpers to Required (1 or 2) or Not required.
  6. Vehicle and extra details (route, second CSM, grid tag, instructions) are optional.
  7. Save. The team gets an in-app and WhatsApp message. The CSM is told a team was assigned.
Busy peopleA person already booked on those dates shows a warning. You can still pick them: saving books them on both jobs (Book anyway as a 2nd job). People on approved leave cannot be picked at all.

Change a job's status Field Jobs › open job

FromYou can move it to
AssignedStarted, On hold, Cancelled
StartedCompleted, On hold, Cancelled
On holdAssigned, Started, Cancelled
CompletedStarted (reopen)
CancelledNothing. Final. Cancelling frees the team's dates. A reason is required.

Details and team can be edited only while the job is Assigned, Started or On hold.

Record or decide leave Leave

  1. Record leave for a Paster, Helper or Driver. Leave you record is approved at once. One record covers at most 120 days.
  2. If the person is on jobs on those days, the system warns you. Tick the box to save anyway, then rearrange the jobs.
  3. Requests from field staff arrive as Pending. Approve (acknowledge any job clash) or Reject with a reason.
  4. To take back approved leave, Cancel it with a reason. Rejected or cancelled leave cannot be edited; record new leave instead.

Log overtime Overtime

  1. Pick up to 5 people, the work date, start time and close time. Optionally link a job.
  2. Hours are worked out from the times. A close time at or before the start time means work ran past midnight.
  3. Save. Entries are Pending until the Admin approves or rejects them. You can edit or delete only pending entries.

Field staff and masters Field Staff · Masters

  • Add a field staff member with name, username, role, mobile, email and password. Mobile and email are required for new accounts.
  • Switch staff off when they leave. Reset passwords when they forget.
  • Keep areas, vehicles and CSM codes correct. Each code, area name and vehicle number must be unique.

Limits

The system will not let you
  • Change orders, billing, users or settings. Those pages are view only for you.
  • Approve or reject overtime. Only the Admin can.
  • Book someone on approved leave, or log overtime on their leave day.
  • Put more than 2 Pasters, 2 Helpers or 1 Driver on a job.
  • Move a job to another order if that order already has payment requests from the job.

Step by step

Create a new order New Order

  1. Enter the client and the Job PO if the customer gave one.
  2. Add a store: store name (for example Fabindia Inorbit Mall), location (for example Madhapur) and Store PO if any.
  3. Add line items to the store: media, width and height in inches, quantity and rate per SFT. SFT and amount are worked out for you.
  4. Add more stores the same way. You can also fill every store at once from an order sheet.
  5. Submit. The order number is given on submit. Accounts and the Admin are told, and you get an email.
TipIf the Admin started the order for you, open it from My Orders and add the stores and lines there.

Upload orders from Excel Upload Orders

  1. Download the template. Fill one order per client. The first date becomes the order date.
  2. Upload the file and check the preview. Rows with problems are marked.
  3. Fix the sheet if needed and upload again. Then confirm to create the orders.

Serial No, Order No, Total SFT and Amount are ignored and worked out. Store Name and Location are saved exactly as typed in their columns.

Correct an order My Orders › open order

  • Header fields (client, PO and so on) can be edited until the order is settled.
  • Stores can be added, renamed or removed until billing starts. Remove a store's lines before removing the store.
  • New lines can be added while the order is Active or Installed.
  • Saved lines cannot be edited or removed. If one is wrong: flag it with a reason (for example Dimensions incorrect) and add a corrected line. The Admin resolves the flag.

Mark a store installed Order › store

  1. Wait until every production item of the store is complete.
  2. Mark that store installed. Only the CSM who owns the order (or the Admin) can do this.
  3. When every store is installed the order becomes Installed and goes to Accounts for billing.

A store with no production work goes straight to billing; it does not need to be marked installed.

Ask for an amount change NEW Order › Amount changes

  1. Press Ask for an amount change. Choose Extra Amount or Less Amount, for one line or for the whole order.
  2. Enter the reason (required) and, if you like, the figure you suggest.
  3. Press Send to admin. The Admin approves (and may change the figure) or rejects with a reason. You are told either way.

Follow up billing, export Order

  • After billing you can add follow-up notes (calls, promises to pay). Accounts sees them.
  • Export your orders to Excel, or email the export to a company email address.

Field jobs on your orders Field Jobs

Jobs where you are the CSM appear here. You can add work details (for example Flex fixing and glow sign installation at 3 stores) and link one of your orders if the job was booked without one. You are told when a team is assigned and when the job is completed.

Claim an expense Payment Requests

  1. Press New payment request. Search your orders. Only orders open to requests can be picked (see the rule below).
  2. Fill Field job (optional), amount, category (Travel, Material, Labour, Food, Advance, Other) and purpose.
  3. Pay to. Your UPI ID (name@bank) or UPI mobile number.
  4. Bill details. Need to submit details goes to Cost Control; the bill must be attached before it can close. No need to submit details goes to the Admin first, then Cost Control.
  5. Attach bills or photos now or later, then submit.
When an order takes requestsAn order opens for requests when all its production items are complete, or it is Installed, or it has no production items. It closes to new requests once the first invoice is raised. Requests already raised carry on.

What can happen to your request

ResultWhat to do
Returned for infoRead the reviewer's reason. Fix the request and Resubmit. If you raise the amount or change the UPI, review starts again from the beginning.
RejectedFinal. Read the reason. Raise a new request if it is right to.

Paid / ClosedCheck your UPI. If the amount is wrong or did not arrive, contact Accounts or support. Payout errors are fixed outside the system.

You can Cancel your own request until someone approves it.

Limits

The system will not let you
  • Edit or delete a saved line item. Flag it and add a corrected line.
  • Add lines or stores after billing starts, or change a settled order. Ask the Admin.
  • Mark a store installed while its production items are open, or on another CSM's order.
  • Set loss remarks or change billed amounts yourself. You ask; the Admin decides.
  • Have more than 50 stores in one order, or two stores with the same name and location.

Step by step

Raise an invoice Billing Queue › open order

  1. Check the order first: stores, lines, remarks and amount changes. Order billable shows the amount that can be billed.
  2. Pick the stores this invoice covers. A store is ready when its production is complete and it is installed, or it has no production work.
  3. Enter Invoice Number (for example SBD/26-27/0142), Billing Date and Bill Amount.
  4. Save. The order becomes Awaiting Payment, or Pending if billed less than the billable amount.

Record a payment Order › invoice

  1. On the invoice, enter Amount Received and Payment Date.
  2. Save. Full payment on every invoice makes the order Payment Received. A shortfall makes it Pending.
  3. For a Pending order, add follow-up notes as you chase the customer. The Admin closes it when settled.

Mistakes: use Edit billing or Edit payment. Each invoice holds one payment record; correct it rather than adding a second.

What can happen

SituationResult
Bill = billable, paid in fullPayment Received. Done.
Billed short or paid shortPending. Follow up; the Admin closes with a remark.
Admin approves an amount change after billingIf the billed total no longer matches, the order moves to Pending and you are told. Check and edit billing.
Only some stores billedRaise further invoices for the remaining stores later.

Limits

The system will not let you
  • Bill more than the billable value of the stores covered.
  • Put one store on two invoices, or reuse an invoice number within an order.
  • Record more received than billed.
  • Change store coverage of an invoice that is already paid.
  • Create or edit orders, or close orders.

Your dashboard

One page holds everything: tiles for Waiting on you, To be paid, Overdue and Paid this month, a view of where requests wait, paid amounts by category, and the request list with filters and search.

Step by step

Review a request Dashboard › open request

  1. Open a request in Cost Control review. Check the order, the order value, total already asked on the order, the payee and the files.
  2. Approve: it moves to Payment pending. Return for info: reason required, it goes back to the requester. Reject: reason required, final.

A request can be approved and paid before its bill arrives. The bill is needed only to close it.

Pay and close Request › Payment pending

  1. Send the money by UPI from the company account, outside the system.
  2. Press Mark paid. Enter the amount paid and the UTR (optional but recommended). Paying less than asked needs a reason.
  3. The requester is told. When the paperwork is complete, press Close.
Before you mark paidIf you find a problem while a request is Payment pending, you can still Reject it with a reason. Once marked paid it cannot be rejected or reversed in the system.

Timers and reminders

Each request shows how long it has waited at its step, in working days. After the overdue limit (default 10 working days) it turns red. Every working day after 09:30 you get one in-app summary of overdue requests waiting on you.

Limits

The system will not let you
  • Act on requests still in Admin review.
  • Pay more than the amount asked.
  • Close a Need to submit details request without a bill attached.
  • Undo a payment. Bounced or wrong payments are handled outside the system with Accounts.
